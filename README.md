PRIVACY POLICY — RESP RESIDENT
Last updated: October 30, 2026

1. Who we are and how to contact us
Resp Resident is developed by AKMD Consulting. Privacy and support questions may be sent to abhisker@gmail.com. This policy describes the current iPhone and iPad app. External websites and Apple services have their own policies.

2. App purpose and accounts
The app is an educational reference intended for trained healthcare professionals and supervised postgraduate trainees. No account, login or registration is required. It is not a patient record, secure clinical messaging service or patient-care decision system.

3. Information processed on your device
Calculator entries are processed temporarily in the current app screen state. They are not saved by the app as patient records or sent by the app to a developer-operated server. Use fictional educational examples and do not enter patient data, identifiers or potentially identifiable case information.
The app stores local preferences for appearance and startup-notice acknowledgment, including the app version and notice revision. These preferences use Apple’s app storage mechanisms. The app does not upload these preferences to a developer-operated server. Device backup, migration or restoration may include local app settings according to your Apple and device settings.

4. Tracking, advertising and analytics
The current app does not include advertising, third-party analytics SDKs, cross-app tracking or a developer-integrated crash-reporting service. The developer does not receive calculator entries through an app backend and does not sell calculator entries or local preferences. Apple may process App Store, device, diagnostic or usage information under its own policies and the choices you make with Apple. This policy does not describe Apple’s independent processing.

5. External links and email
The app can open professional guidelines, journals, government resources, product information, the App Store and other destinations in system apps or a browser. Those operators may receive your IP address, browser/device details and interaction information under their own policies. Their practices are outside the developer’s control.
If you choose Email Developer or otherwise contact us, your chosen email service sends your email address, message and any attachments to the developer’s email service. You choose whether and what to send. Do not send patient information or confidential institutional material. Opening a mail link does not itself send a message; you control sending in your email app.

6. Support correspondence
We use correspondence you choose to send to respond to questions, investigate reported problems and consider requests, including requests to add an institution. Such messages may be retained in the developer’s email service as reasonably necessary for these purposes or applicable legal obligations. Contact us to ask about access, correction or deletion of correspondence; requests are handled subject to applicable law and necessary retention. Do not include patient information in a privacy request.

7. Local settings and deletion
You can change supported preferences within the app. Deleting the app normally removes its local app data, although backup or device restoration may restore settings. Manage backups through your Apple/device settings. There is no app account to delete. To ask about correspondence held by the developer, email the contact address above.

8. Security and confidentiality
The app is not designed or authorized to store or transmit confidential patient information. Follow your institution’s device-security and privacy requirements. Screenshots, shared devices, device compromise, backups or external services may expose information independently of the app. No security measure can guarantee complete protection. If information is entered or shared inadvertently, follow the applicable institutional privacy and incident-reporting process.

9. Children
This app is intended for adult healthcare professionals and supervised postgraduate trainees, not children. The developer does not knowingly solicit children’s personal information through the app. Contact us if you believe a child has sent personal information to the developer.

10. Your choices and applicable rights
Using external links or emailing the developer is optional. This policy explains current practices; it is not a waiver of privacy rights. Where consent is required by applicable law, it must be obtained appropriately and does not waive other legal obligations. Contact us about privacy questions or applicable access, correction and deletion rights. Rights and available remedies depend on the applicable law.

11. Changes
We may update this policy when the app or its practices change. The revised policy will show a new last-updated date. Material changes may also be brought to your attention within the app. Review the current policy after updates.
